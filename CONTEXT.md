# renewvan Hub

An open-hardware, vendor-agnostic campervan control platform: a unified device model for van hardware (tanks, relays, batteries, solar controllers, chargers, routers), published on a single MQTT bus by drivers, consumed by a dashboard and logging. Almost everything on the bus is read-only; the exceptions are the settings and command topics (`<field>/set`) such as the **Relay** `set`. See `review/renewvan-hub-research-report.md` for the full research/architecture rationale, and `.scratch/renewvan-hub-v0/map.md` for the v0 spec effort.

## Language

**Entity**:
A category of van hardware in the unified device model (`tank`, `relay`, `heater`, `battery`, `solar`, `charger`, `router`, `gps`, `temperature`, `tilt`), each with a field list published on the MQTT bus. The machine-checkable contract for each entity's fields is the JSON Schema under [`/schema`](schema/) (`tank.schema.json`, `relay.schema.json`, `heater.schema.json`, `battery.schema.json`, `solar.schema.json`, `charger.schema.json`, `router.schema.json`, `gps.schema.json`, `temperature.schema.json`, `tilt.schema.json`).
An entity is named for what the hardware _is_, never for a brand or protocol (`battery`, not `victron-battery`): the schema is where vendor-neutrality lives. Each schema requires only the properties every source can supply and marks the rest optional, so consumers must tolerate an optional property being absent. Every entity instance may also carry two optional retained identity properties: `driver` (the key of the **Driver** that publishes it, `renewvan/<entity>/<id>/driver`) and `name` (its human label, `renewvan/<entity>/<id>/name`); a consumer without `driver` attributes the instance to every Driver whose info `publishes` that entity. See `docs/adr/0008-node-naming-and-entity-neutrality.md` and `docs/adr/0019-entity-instances-name-their-driver-and-shared-primary-battery.md`.
Every entity must also have a demo scenario in `schema/demo/`, so it shows up in **Demo mode**: see `docs/demo-mode.md`.
_Avoid_: Thing, component (too generic — use the specific entity name); Device (the hardware itself, not the publisher; the publisher of an entity is a **Driver**).

**Renewvan bus**:
The single MQTT broker every driver publishes to and every consumer (dashboard, logger) reads from, under the `renewvan/<domain>/<id>/<property>` topic convention.
_Avoid_: Message bus, broker (when the campervan-specific bus is meant, not MQTT generically); "Van bus" (superseded — the topic root is `renewvan`, not `van`).

**Renewvan hub**:
The Docker Compose stack running on the Pi that forms the connective core of the platform: the Mosquitto broker (renewvan bus), pinned driver containers, and plugins. From a user's perspective, "Renewvan hub connected" means the dashboard has an active MQTT-over-WebSocket connection to this stack. From an operator's perspective, "the hub" refers to the Pi-side compose deployment managed by this repo.
_Avoid_: "Bus connected" (ambiguous — sounds like a vehicle CAN/RV-C bus or the MQTT protocol itself, not the Pi service); "hub service" (the hub is the whole stack, not one service inside it).

**Tank**:
A fluid reservoir (fresh water, grey water, black water, fuel, or LPG) monitored by a resistive level sensor. Reports `fluid_type`, `capacity_l`, `level_pct` (raw, fill percentage not volume), `status`, and `volume_since_full_l`/`volume_since_empty_l`, plus optionally `alarm_state`/`alarm_direction`/`alarm_threshold_pct`/`alarm_restore_pct`, `temperature_c`, and the `last_inspected_at`/`last_full_at`/`last_empty_at` timestamps.
_Avoid_: Reservoir.

**Relay**:
A writable on/off load, one per channel (a light circuit, a pump, a heater circuit that is only switched on or off; a heater that reports its own phase and faults is a **Heater**), whatever the board that switches it. Always reports `state`, a boolean meaning the output was commanded and latched, **not** that the load is energised: with no feedback sensor a stuck relay or blown fuse is not detected, and no consumer may claim otherwise. Optionally reports `name`, the only label on the wire; grouping and icons are a consumer's presentation concern and never on the wire. Commands are `renewvan/relay/<id>/set` (JSON `true`/`false`, never a toggle) and `renewvan/relay/<id>/name/set` (a JSON string), both unretained. After any restart every channel is off, and a consumer treats `state` as unknown while the owning Driver is not `online`. See `docs/adr/0013-writable-relay-and-its-wire-contract.md`.
_Avoid_: Switch, load (relay is the wire-schema entity name); "is on" for what `state` says (it means latched, not energised).

**Heater**:
A fuel-fired space heater that reports its own operating **phase** (`off`, `starting`, `warming_up`, `running`, `shutting_down`) and faults, whatever the brand, one entity per heater (first source: an Autoterm AIR 2D read directly over UART by `renewvan/driver-autoterm`, sensing, not a feed remap). Distinct from a **Relay**, which only latches an output and knows nothing about the load. `phase` is what the heater reports; whether it is also ventilating (fan only) is a separate derived boolean. Writable, with commands that are never re-sent by the Driver after a restart; thermal cutoffs stay in the heater's own hardware, never in software. Fields carry the unit in their name, like every other entity. The contract is `schema/heater.schema.json` and `docs/adr/0021-writable-heater-and-its-command-safety-rules.md`.
_Avoid_: Autoterm, Webasto (brands, not the entity name); `state` for the phase (on **Relay**, `state` means latched, not what the load is doing); Furnace.

**Battery**:
A battery bank as seen by a shunt, BMS, or inverter-charger, whatever the vendor. Always reports `soc_pct`, `voltage_v`, `current_a`, `power_w`; optionally `capacity_ah` and `remaining_ah` (a source with a fuel gauge reports both as its own raw values, so consumers can cross-check `soc_pct` against `remaining_ah / capacity_ah`), `temperature_c` (only with a probe), `time_to_go_s` (present whenever the source reports a usable estimate, regardless of charge state; absent when it reports none), `time_since_full_charge_s`, and `charge_state` (only from a source that knows the charge stage — a plain shunt does not). Sourced from Venus OS via `renewvan/driver-venus` (`system/0/Batteries`, id = Venus device instance, a shunt or inverter-charger) and, directly from the BMS over BLE, via `renewvan/driver-wattcycle`; any other source publishes the same entity from its own Driver. Each instance may carry `driver` and `name` (see **Entity**). One physical bank can therefore appear as two instances, one per source: both stay separate `battery` instances (selection, not fusion), and the **Primary battery** picks which one is the headline.
A BMS-backed instance may also carry its pack's own data, all optional and retained: `cell_voltages_v` (one array, one entry per cell, `null` for an unreadable cell) with `cell_voltage_max_v`/`min_v`/`delta_v`, `cell_balancing` (index-aligned with the cells), `temperatures_c` (one entry per probe), `allow_charge`/`allow_discharge` (the BMS currently permits charging/discharging), `protections` (the active protections, a fixed vendor-neutral vocabulary; `[]` = none active, absent = not reported) and source-reported `charge_voltage_limit_v`, `charge_current_limit_a`, `discharge_current_limit_a`. One instance carries at most one BMS's cells, never merged across packs, and a consumer never borrows cells from a sibling instance. See `docs/adr/0022-per-cell-bms-data-lives-on-battery.md`.
_Avoid_: Shunt, BMS (the underlying hardware, not this project's entity name; a BMS's per-cell data is `battery` data, not a separate `bms` entity).

**Primary battery** (`renewvan/hub/primary-battery`):
The one **Battery** instance every client treats as the headline battery when a bank has more than one source. A shared setting, not a per-client preference: a retained JSON string on `renewvan/hub/primary-battery` that is either `"auto"` (deterministic: sort instance ids, first complete battery) or a battery instance id, changed by publishing the same payload, unretained, to `renewvan/hub/primary-battery/set`. Owned by the **Hub plugin**, which persists it on the Pi and does not check it against the instances on the bus; a consumer whose chosen instance is absent or incomplete falls back to `auto` and says so. Schema: `schema/hub-primary-battery.schema.json`. See `docs/adr/0019-entity-instances-name-their-driver-and-shared-primary-battery.md`.
_Avoid_: Main battery, default battery, selected battery (per-client wording); a fused or averaged reading of two sources (selection, not fusion).

**Solar**:
A solar charge controller (MPPT/PWM), whatever the vendor, one entity per controller. Always reports `power_w` and `battery_voltage_v`; optionally `battery_current_a`, `pv_voltage_v`, `pv_current_a`, `yield_today_wh`, `yield_history_wh` (the controller's own daily yield log as one retained array, newest first, index 0 = today, `null` = a day with no data; see `docs/adr/0009-solar-yield-history-as-one-retained-array.md`), and the controller's own `charge_state`. Sourced from Venus OS `solarcharger/<instance>/*` via `renewvan/driver-venus`.
_Avoid_: MPPT, PV (hardware/jargon, not the entity name); `charger` (that entity is for non-solar chargers).

**Charger**:
A non-solar battery charger, whatever the vendor: an AC mains charger (e.g. a Victron BlueSmart) or a DC-DC charger fed by the alternator (e.g. an Orion). Only `voltage_v` is required; optionally `current_a`, `power_w`, `input_voltage_v`, `source` (`ac`/`alternator`/`dc`) and `charge_state`. Sourced from Venus OS `charger/<instance>` and `alternator/<instance>` via `renewvan/driver-venus`.
_Avoid_: Booster, orion (a product line, and "booster" names no vendor-neutral thing); alternator charger (that is a `source` value, not the entity).

**Router**:
The van's uplink router — the single device connecting the van to the internet — reporting connectivity status: `signal_rsrp_dbm`/`signal_rsrq_db`/`signal_sinr_db`/`signal_rssi_dbm`, `operator`, `network_type`, `uptime_s`, and monthly SIM data usage (`data_used_month_tx_b`/`data_used_month_rx_b`). Sourced from the router vendor's push feed via the `renewvan/driver-router` driver, like the Venus driver remaps an existing feed.
_Avoid_: Gateway, modem (hardware inside the router, not this entity), RUT (vendor name), WAN (networking jargon — the entity's field is `network_type`).

**GPS**:
Live position telemetry (no identity/startup-only fields) from a USB GPS/GLONASS/BeiDou module read directly by the `renewvan/driver-gps` driver (NMEA 0183 over serial — sensing, not a feed remap). Reports `fix_quality`, `satellites_in_use`, `avg_snr_db` unconditionally (`avg_snr_db` is independent of fix status — mean per-satellite signal strength from GSV sentences, present even before a fix); `latitude`, `longitude`, `altitude_m`, `hdop`, `speed_kmh`, `course_deg`, `fix_time` only once `fix_quality != no_fix`. Optionally reports `satellites`, one retained array of every satellite in view (`constellation`, `prn`, `snr_db`, `elevation_deg`, `azimuth_deg`; `snr_db` is `null`, never `0`, for a satellite in view without a measurement yet), also independent of fix status and not the same population as `satellites_in_use`. See `docs/adr/0017-gps-satellites-as-one-retained-array.md`.
_Avoid_: Location, position (too generic — use the entity name); GNSS (the schema/topic name is `gps` even though the module also tracks GLONASS/BeiDou).

**Temperature**:
A standalone temperature sensor (1-Wire DS18B20 probe or the Pi's own SoC thermal zone) read directly by the `renewvan/driver-temperature` driver (sensing, not a feed remap), keyed by a user-assigned sensor id. Reports `name`, `unit`, `source` (and `serial` for 1-Wire) as retained identity fields, and `temperature_c`/`status` live. `temperature_c` is always Celsius on the wire; `unit` (`C`/`F`) is a display preference the dashboard applies. `name` and `unit` are user-editable via `<field>/set` command topics and persisted driver-side, overriding the `config.ini` default. Temperatures that belong to another entity's hardware (tank water temperature, battery temperature) stay on that entity's own `temperature_c` and are not republished here.
_Avoid_: Thermometer, probe (the hardware, not the entity); sensor (too generic, see Entity); publishing the same reading under two entities.

**Tilt**:
Van inclination from a GY-521 / MPU-6050 accelerometer read directly by the `renewvan/driver-tilt` driver (sensing, not a feed remap). Live fields only, no identity fields: `roll_deg` (positive = left side down), `pitch_deg` (positive = nose up), both in the van frame (x forward, y left, z up), smoothed and corrected by an install-time **level zero** captured via `renewvan/tilt/<id>/level_zero/set` (`reset` clears it) and persisted driver-side; `status` (`ok`/`sensor_error`). Whether the van counts as "level" is a tolerance the dashboard applies — the driver publishes angles, not a level flag.
_Avoid_: Level, inclination, slope (the entity/topic name is `tilt`); an `is_level` boolean on the wire (a presentation threshold would force a driver release to retune).

**Driver**:
The software that publishes one specific source onto the renewvan bus, normalized into the device model: firmware on an ESP32 (the relay driver) or a process on the Pi (the tank driver), whether it owns the sensing (the tank driver's ADS1115 math) or remaps an _existing_ external feed (the Venus driver, remapping Venus OS's MQTT feed onto `renewvan/battery|solar|charger/<id>/...`). A Driver is named for its _source_ (`driver-venus`, `driver-wattcycle`), not for the entity it publishes: one Driver may publish several entities, and several Drivers may publish the same entity (a battery bank read by both `driver-venus` and `driver-wattcycle`). Its key is the repo name minus `driver-` (`driver-esp32` is the one Driver `esp32`; its firmware variants only select which firmware is built) and is the `<driver>` segment of `renewvan/<driver>/info` and `renewvan/<driver>/health`, and the value of the optional `driver` property on each entity instance it publishes, which is how an instance names its owner. The firmware of an ESP32 board is a Driver that is also its own hardware (Driver `esp32`, from the `driver-esp32` repo, which today ships the 16-channel relay firmware); the Settings list shows Drivers, not the hardware they read. `driver-` is also the artifact prefix of a Driver's repo, image and package, so concept and artifact agree. See `docs/adr/0018-device-to-driver-rename.md`.
_Avoid_: Node (superseded, see `docs/adr/0014-node-to-device-rename.md`), Device (now means hardware: a battery, a router, a USB module; never the publisher), Bridge (a Driver that remaps an existing feed is still a Driver), Thing, Component (too generic).

**Board**:
The physical hardware a Driver runs on (the Raspberry Pi, an ESP32 dev board, a wall panel). One board may run several Drivers; a Driver is never a board.
_Avoid_: Host (superseded), Device (hardware in general, ambiguous with the hardware a Driver reads), module (a third party's word for its own hardware).

**Driver manifest**:
The static description of one **Driver**, written once by its author as `manifest.json` at the root of the Driver's repo: what the Driver is, what it reads, and which entity types it publishes. It never carries a version or a Board serial. Contract: `schema/driver-manifest.schema.json`. See `docs/adr/0011-node-manifest-and-retained-info-topic.md`.
_Avoid_: Config, descriptor, metadata (too generic).

**Driver info**:
What a **Driver** publishes about itself, retained, at `renewvan/<driver>/info`: its **Driver manifest** plus the version it is running and, when known, the serial and the current IPv4 address (`ip`, only a Driver with an address of its own, such as an ESP32 on WiFi) of its **Board**. It stays on the bus when the Driver goes offline. Not an entity: it has no `<id>`. Contract: `schema/driver-info.schema.json`. See `docs/adr/0011-node-manifest-and-retained-info-topic.md`.
_Avoid_: Driver status, heartbeat (that is the separate `online`/`offline` health word); Driver metadata.

**Demo mode**:
A simulated van on the renewvan bus, so the dashboard (and any other consumer) can be built and shown without a van or hardware. Modelled on Venus OS's Demo mode. One switch shared by every client: any client publishes `renewvan/demo/enabled/set`, **driver-demo** answers with the retained `renewvan/demo/enabled` and starts or clears the simulated van, and every dashboard follows that flag (off by default). Real drivers and the logger keep running, and the dashboard ignores the real van while it is on. Nothing simulated is ever persisted. Read-only: command topics are not simulated. See `docs/demo-mode.md` and `docs/adr/0010-demo-mode-publishes-under-renewvan-demo.md`.
_Avoid_: Simulation mode, test mode, mock mode.

**Demo namespace** (`renewvan/demo/`):
The topic namespace `driver-demo` publishes under, one level inside the real root: `renewvan/demo/<entity>/<id>/<property>`, with the same property names, JSON encoding and retained flag, plus `renewvan/demo/health` (`online`/`offline`, LWT) and the shared switch `renewvan/demo/enabled` (+ `/set`). It is inside `renewvan/#`, so every subscriber of the real root receives it. That is safe for the logger because it only writes four-segment topics whose domain is an entity, and a demo entity topic has five segments; `demo` is never an entity domain. Never published on a real entity topic, because real retained state would be overwritten.
_Avoid_: Demo root, demo bus, demo broker (it is the same broker and a sub-namespace of the same root), `renewvan-demo/` (the first version's sibling root, superseded by ADR 0010).

**Demo scenario** (`schema/demo/<entity>[.<name>].demo.json`):
A hub-owned file describing one simulated entity instance: an `initial` record plus a `timeline` of `set` overlays over a `loop_s` loop, validated by `schema/validate-demo.py` against the entity's schema. Scenarios are per entity, not per source, and share one clock so entities stay coherent. Venus analogue: a `dbus-recorder` recording.
_Avoid_: Fixture (those are `schema/examples/`, static valid/invalid payloads), recording (nothing is captured live), mock.

**Demo fleet** (`schema/demo/fleet.demo-fleet.json`):
A hub-owned file declaring the simulated **Drivers** of a demo van: each entry is a complete **Driver info** plus the health to show, optionally a health timeline over the shared `loop_s` loop, and no info at all to simulate a Driver that has not been updated. Versions and serials in it are visibly fake. It is separate from the **Demo scenarios** (which are per entity) and is validated by the fleet pass of `schema/validate-demo.py`. See `docs/demo-mode.md`.
_Avoid_: Fixture (those are `schema/examples/`), scenario (that is per entity), mock fleet.

**driver-demo**:
The process that replays every **Demo scenario** and the **Demo fleet** into the **Demo namespace**. A Pi-side process with an LWT health topic that always runs, idle until `renewvan/demo/enabled` is `true`, so a real van shows nothing fake unless someone switches it on. It is a simulator, not a **Driver**: it owns no hardware, remaps no feed, and has no **Driver manifest** or **Driver info** of its own, so it is named for what it is, not for a source. Its repo keeps the `driver-` artifact prefix for historical reasons only, so the name is a known wart: do not read it as a Driver.
_Avoid_: Mock driver, fake driver, simulator driver (it is none of them: it is the simulator).

**Naming convention**:
Repos are named plainly, scoped by the `renewvan` GitHub org — not a stuttering `renewvan-<name>`; the org already provides the namespace, so re-prefixing every repo restates it. Exception: Driver repos carry a `driver-` role prefix (`renewvan/driver-tank`, `renewvan/driver-venus`), because a bare `tank` collides with the **Entity** name the moment repo, image, and env var are referenced side by side; that prefix is also the concept's name, so a repo, image and package named `driver-<source>` is a **Driver** (the one repo with the prefix that is not, **driver-demo**, is the simulator). `logger`, `dashboard`, and `mobile` aren't Drivers, so they stay bare. All ESP32 firmware lives in one repo, `driver-esp32`, which is one Driver, `esp32`, keyed like every other repo; its firmware variants (today `esp32-16x-relay`) only select the firmware and are not Drivers: see `docs/adr/0020-one-driver-esp32-repo-many-firmware-variants.md`.

**Kiosk plugin** (`plugins/kiosk`):
A Pi-host process (systemd service `renewvan-kiosk`, not a Docker container) in `plugins/kiosk/` that bridges the renewvan bus to OS-level backlight control via the `bl_power` sysfs interface. Subscribes to `renewvan/kiosk/display/power/set`; publishes current state retained to `renewvan/kiosk/display/power`. `power/set` applies unconditionally — no remote/local gating, see `docs/kiosk-display-sleep-wake.md`'s Design decisions for why a prior remote-sleep-allowed gate was removed. Also monitors the touch device via evdev and wakes the display on any touch event while the backlight is off, and drives an opt-in auto-sleep idle timer off the same touch stream, scoped to host-local sessions only (`_display_on_origin`). Also owns brightness (`renewvan/kiosk/display/brightness`[`/set`], the sibling backlight sysfs file to `bl_power`, normalized 0-100 on the wire). See `plugins/kiosk/README.md` for the full topic table.
_Avoid_: "kiosk daemon", "display service", "node-kiosk" / "node_kiosk" (deprecated directory, file, and service names).

**Tailscale plugin** (`plugins/tailscale`):
A Pi-host process (systemd service `renewvan-tailscale`) in `plugins/tailscale/` that connects the Pi to a Tailscale tailnet and publishes VPN status retained to `renewvan/tailscale/status`. Payload fields: `enabled` (daemon running), `connected` (authenticated + online), `ip` (Tailscale IPv4), `hostname`, `peers` (online peer count). Ships installed by `bin/deploy.sh`; requires one-time `sudo tailscale up` to authenticate.
_Avoid_: "VPN device", "Tailscale device" (plugins are not MQTT drivers — they run on the Pi host but have a different contract).

**Basemap** (`/maps/basemap.pmtiles`):
The single `.pmtiles` file the dashboard's Map page draws offline, covering one region. The user builds it on their own machine and copies it to the Pi; the hub only provides the plumbing: the `DASHBOARD_BASEMAP_PATH` key (default `/etc/renewvan/dashboard/basemap.pmtiles`), a single-file read-only mount into the `dashboard` service, an empty placeholder created by `bin/deploy.sh` when no file exists, and `scripts/push-basemap.sh` to replace it safely. A future Pi-side download plugin would write to the same path. The dashboard's offline-map guide owns how to build one.
_Avoid_: "extract" (that is the `pmtiles extract` operation that can produce one, not the file), "tiles" alone (too generic: the file holds many), "map file".

**Hub plugin** (`plugins/hub`):
A Pi-host process (systemd service `renewvan-hub`) in `plugins/hub/` that also owns the shared **Primary battery** setting (persisted on the Pi, republished retained on `renewvan/hub/primary-battery`, written through `renewvan/hub/primary-battery/set`) and publishes the Pi's own local-network address and the hub's version retained to `renewvan/hub/status` (`schema/hub-status.schema.json`). Payload fields: `ip` (source IPv4 of the Pi's default route, so never a Docker bridge or the Tailscale address; `null` with no route), `hostname`, and `version` (the hub release the Pi was deployed from: `git describe --tags --always --dirty` of the deploying checkout, stamped into a `VERSION` file by `bin/deploy.sh` because the Pi has no `.git`; `dev` when unstamped — same convention as **Driver info** `version`). Read from the Pi itself, so the hub's IP is on the bus whether or not a router Driver is installed — the router entity carries no IP. Ships installed by `bin/deploy.sh`; nothing to authenticate.
_Avoid_: "hub device", "router IP" (the router entity has no address; this is the Pi's), "broker address" (what a client happens to connect to, which can be a Tailscale name or a proxy, not the Pi's LAN address).

**Display power state** (`renewvan/kiosk/display/power`):
A retained MQTT topic carrying `"on"` or `"off"` (see `schema/kiosk-display-power.schema.json`). Published by the kiosk plugin after each successful backlight `bl_power` write, and initialised from the live hardware state on startup. The dashboard reflects it via `DisplaySleepButton`'s icon only — no on-screen "sleeping" overlay (removed, see `docs/kiosk-display-sleep-wake.md`'s Design decisions: redundant with the physical backlight cut and with `kiosk.py`'s own touch grab, and it needed increasingly complex machinery to avoid also rendering on remote viewers). The only visible effect of `"off"` is the Pi's own backlight cutting. The matching command topic (`renewvan/kiosk/display/power/set`) carries the same payload but is not retained.

**Driver artifact naming** (blanket rule, not a per-repo call): a driver's repo, Docker image, and `hub`-side pin all carry the same role qualifier. Pattern: repo `renewvan/driver-<source>`, image `ghcr.io/renewvan/driver-<source>`, where `<source>` is the entity for a driver that owns the sensing of one kind of hardware (`driver-tank`, `driver-tilt`) and the protocol or brand for a driver that bridges a source (`driver-venus`); see `docs/adr/0008-node-naming-and-entity-neutrality.md`. ESP32 firmware follows the rule too: the single repo `driver-esp32` is the one Driver `esp32`, and its firmware variants (`esp32-16x-relay` for the 16-channel relay board) are file, ESPHome node and project names, not Driver keys; see `docs/adr/0020-one-driver-esp32-repo-many-firmware-variants.md` (which supersedes the board-named rule of `docs/adr/0012-board-named-relay-node.md` and the repo name of `docs/adr/0016-esp32-16x-relay-rename.md`). The role prefix comes first in every artifact name. `logger` and `dashboard` are exempt (bare `renewvan/logger`, `renewvan/dashboard`). The `hub`-side pin is the `image:` tag in `docker-compose.yml` itself (git-tracked, no env var indirection — see `docs/adr/0001-compose-services-via-pinned-images-not-git-submodules.md`), not an `.env` value.
